# Design critique — 2026-09-28

An independent design critique (Opus subagent, no access to this project's conversation
history, viewing the live rendered site on both layouts) of `index.html` against
`CONTEXT.md`. Two passes: the first pass was briefed with a wrong concept ("Vietnamese
lacquerware") that isn't this project's actual axis — that mistake invalidated its original
composition finding #1, which is why this doc's Level 1 section is the *revised* version,
re-run against the real `CONTEXT.md` brief. Level 2 (component-level) findings don't depend
on the concept and were only gathered once.

Checkboxes track whether a finding has been addressed. A checked box links to what changed;
unchecked findings are still open. On 2026-09-28 a first pass fixed the low-risk items (bugs,
contrast, isolated spacing/content) that don't touch layout, motifs, or section structure —
everything structural in Level 1 is still open.

## Level 1 — whole-page composition

1. [ ] **HIGH — Section headers no longer follow the kit.** `CONTEXT.md` documents one header
   frame (cream signboard, double keyline, corner rivets). In the live site each section
   built its own: Story is a newsstand with a newspaper clipping, Schedule is a cinema
   marquee with bulbs, Venue is a tilted red "tower board," Dress is a tailor's sign with
   scissors, FAQ is a manila notice with a pin/staples/"APPROVED" stamp, Gallery is a
   photo-lab label, RSVP is a red plaque. Only Story/Schedule/Dress are even close to the
   documented panel. This is the same "each justified alone, noisy together" failure the
   "Divider consolidation" entry below already fixed once — it resurfaced one level up, at
   headers instead of seams, and headers are the landmarks guests actually scan by.
   *Direction:* one header component built to the kit spec; let sections vary only by a
   kicker line or a single icon.

2. [ ] **HIGH — The chosen display face barely carries the headings.** `CONTEXT.md` says Hồng Ký
   1 was chosen over Chi Son/Hien Khanh/others after a live diacritic stress test. In the
   live site, headings are mostly set in the two rejected sibling faces: Chi Son ("Order of
   the Evening," "The Venue," "Questions & Answers," "Moments," the Gazette masthead) and
   Hien Khanh ("Our Story," "You're invited," "RS/VP," every script kicker). Hồng Ký 1 only
   appears in schedule times, FAQ questions, the vertical hero banner, and "N&N" details.
   This isn't a re-litigation of which face to use — it's that the winning choice isn't
   actually applied where it matters most.
   *Direction:* set every section heading and the names in Hồng Ký 1; keep at most one
   secondary script, only for small kickers.

3. [ ] **MEDIUM — Red/gold used beyond their documented accent role, which is where the
   contrast failures come from.** `CONTEXT.md`: gold is "a fine accent (rules, small
   details) only," red is signage lettering, not a field. Live: red is a full field on the
   Venue header, the RSVP plaque, and the hero's vertical banner, with gold as large display
   text on top of it — directly producing the 1.81:1 ("You're invited"), 2.48:1 ("You'll
   find us at"), and 3.23:1 (vertical banner) contrast failures from the component-level
   pass below. The rest of the palette (marigold/Venue, palm green/Schedule+Gallery+footer,
   ivory elsewhere) does follow the brief; palm green genuinely works as a third field. One
   miss: the brief lists the hero as a marigold/ivory field, but it's a full-bleed photo
   with no marigold in it.
   *Direction:* header lettering in lacquer red on the cream panel, as documented; gold
   stays on rules/small details only.
   *Partial:* the contrast failures this produced are fixed (see Level 2) by recoloring the
   text to ivory — red is still used as a full field, not yet pulled back to the documented
   accent-only role.

4. [ ] **MEDIUM — The venue axis (architecture half of the brief) is nearly invisible.** Door
   arch: documented for the hero frame, photo masks, and RSVP card; live, it's only the
   Story photo mask, a small venue-postcard badge, and a faint back-cover emboss — no hero
   frame, no RSVP card (that's an airmail envelope instead). Merlon: present at all six
   documented seams, correctly — but drawn with a rounded top (`M7,22 L7,12 Q15,4 23,12
   L23,22`) instead of the documented "pointed" profile, so on screen it reads as a postage
   stamp's perforated edge / scallop rather than a roofline. Since stamps/postcards/airmail
   stripes are already all over the page, the one venue-axis seam device blends into the
   paper-goods look instead of standing apart from it.
   *Direction:* give the merlon a point; put the door arch back on the hero frame and RSVP
   card.

5. [ ] **MEDIUM — The Dress→FAQ seam is a gap in the brief's own rule.** `CONTEXT.md` says that
   seam needs no merlon because both sides are ivory. But Dress has the full-bleed gạch bông
   tile backdrop, so the tile still hard-cuts against plain ivory at that boundary.
   *Direction:* the seam rule should key off "the surface changes," not "the color changes"
   — add an ivory merlon there, or fade the tile out.

6. [ ] **MEDIUM — Photo treatment is uneven.** Brief calls for film grain + warm fade on
   photography, site-wide. Grain is only defined once, on the hero's vignette. The venue
   photo is saturated/clean (reads as stock photography); photobook photos read as
   untreated phone selfies; the hero is warmer than both.
   *Direction:* one shared grain + warm-fade treatment applied to every photo (hero, Story,
   venue, photobook).

7. [x] **HIGH — Section order buries the emotional payoff and the one action the site
   exists for.** Current order: Hero → Story → Schedule → Venue → Dress → FAQ → Gallery →
   RSVP. Gallery (the most emotional, interactive section) lands after FAQ, where guests
   have switched into task mode; RSVP is last, behind a ~900px photobook. The site is for
   friends/coworkers who already know the couple, and Story is framed as "an office
   romance" — Story and Gallery belong together as one warm opening.
   *Direction:* Hero → Story → Gallery → Schedule → Venue → Dress → RSVP → FAQ (FAQ as
   reference material after the action, not before it).
   *Fixed 2026-09-28:* sections reordered to exactly this sequence. Seam merlons re-derived
   from the "entering section's own colour" rule ([[Divider consolidation]]): Gallery keeps
   its `c-palm` merlon entering from Story (ivory); Gallery→Schedule is now a same-colour
   (palm→palm) seam so Schedule's old `c-palm` merlon was removed; Schedule→Venue and
   Venue→Dress merlons are unchanged; Dress→RSVP is now a same-colour (ivory→ivory) seam so
   RSVP's old `c-ivory` merlon was removed; RSVP→FAQ stays seamless (both ivory, as
   Dress→FAQ used to be); FAQ→footer keeps the existing `c-palm` footer merlon. Verified
   end-to-end in-browser (all seams, photobook, RSVP form). Nav link order untouched — Our
   Story/Schedule/Venue/FAQ is still a valid subsequence of the new order.
   *Follow-up 2026-09-29:* the reorder put two same-colour band pairs back to back
   (Gallery→Schedule, both palm; Dress→RSVP→FAQ, three ivory in a row) which read as rough —
   merlon only fires on a colour change. Fixed by swapping Schedule (palm→ivory) and RSVP
   (ivory→palm) instead of adding a second seam device; every seam now changes colour, so
   all seven get a merlon with no new CSS. `CONTEXT.md` updated to match.

8. [ ] **HIGH — The venue address is hidden behind "Tap to flip."** At rest the venue card
   shows only a photo and "Chloe Gallery — District 7." Address, transport, parking, map are
   all on the back face behind a small flip affordance — the single most-needed piece of
   information on the day, on a phone. (The flip-postcard treatment itself is also
   undocumented — see drift table.)
   *Direction:* address + a "Directions" button on the front face, not behind a flip.

9. [ ] **MEDIUM — Desktop wastes ~65% of the width.** Every section uses a centered
   ~440–560px column (gazette, radio card, postcard, dress card, FAQ board, RSVP envelope)
   with large empty margins on both sides at 1440px — the desktop layout reads as the
   mobile layout centered on a wide screen.
   *Direction:* real two-column desktop compositions per section (Story photo+text side by
   side, Schedule as a horizontal timeline, Venue details beside the photo instead of
   flipped, etc.)

10. [ ] **MEDIUM — Pacing repeats one beat with no crescendo.** Title card → one content card
    → band change, every section, with a blank gap right after the mobile hero (a "SCROLL"
    label followed by ~110px of empty ivory). Unifying the headers (finding 1) would make
    this repetition read as calm rhythm instead of visual noise.
    *Direction:* drop the post-hero gap/SCROLL label; let one section (Gallery or Venue) go
    full-bleed for scale variation.

11. [ ] **LOW — No section navigation on mobile; RSVP CTA is split** between the nav button and
    the hero's "RS / VP" diamond (whose line break reads as two words). Desktop nav also
    omits Dress Code and Gallery.

12. [x] **LOW — Name-separator glyph disagrees with the doc.** `CONTEXT.md` specifies ◆ between
    names and in section labels. Live: ♥ (`heart-sep`) is used 35 times, ✦ 8 times, ◆ only 4
    times — commit `90de60a` ("Replace diamond separators with heart motif") changed this
    without updating `CONTEXT.md`.
    *Direction:* pick one glyph, use it everywhere, update whichever side (doc or site) is
    wrong.
    *Fixed 2026-09-28:* `CONTEXT.md` now documents ♥ as the separator (site was already
    consistent) and notes the commit that changed it.

**Recommendation from the critique:** consolidate headers the same way the seams were
consolidated — one painted sign panel everywhere; keep the in-section objects doing real
work (Bảng thông báo, photobook, possibly airmail RSVP if reframed onto the door-arch card);
cut the gazette, radio, marquee, tower board, leftover manila/stamp sign, and fireflies;
update `CONTEXT.md` in the same pass so the doc and the site agree again.

## Reference: "Divider consolidation" precedent

`CONTEXT.md` already documents fixing this exact failure mode once, at the seams instead of
the headers — see its "Divider consolidation (2026-09-14)" entry: three separately-justified
transition motifs (gạch bông as bookends, kangura merlon at seams, cửa xếp under the nav)
read as noise together despite each having its own rationale, resolved by making merlon the
only seam device. This critique's top finding is that the same pattern needs to happen again,
one level up.
